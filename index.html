Real-time audio–visual fall detection system for indoor safety monitoring.

Developed a robust multimodal detection pipeline and an interactive monitoring dashboard that enables real-time status tracking and alert feedback. The system focuses on temporal reasoning and reliability under noisy inputs, supporting flexible downstream notification modules.

Multi-view 3D Human Motion Analysis for Indoor Fitness

Multi-view 3D reconstruction and temporal modeling for repetition counting and abnormal-motion detection.

Led the 3D component of an indoor fitness motion analysis pipeline. The system reconstructs temporally consistent motion from multi-view observations and supports human-centric temporal analytics such as repetition counting and abnormal-motion detection. We adopted SMPL-X as the 3D human representation and focused on robustness under real-world indoor variability.

PyTorch Multi-view geometry Temporal modeling SMPL-X
